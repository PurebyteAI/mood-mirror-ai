# Mood Mirror AI — Update

This version answers the product review: the reflection is a short poem about what the person actually said, the picture shows that same moment, and the result is laid out so it can later be printed.

## Reflection poem

- The mirror answers with a poem of two stanzas, four lines each.
- The tone is funny and optimistic, and the lines use the person's own situation.
- A follow-up question writes a new poem about that question.

## Generated image

- The picture is built from the words the person entered, not a generic landscape.
- It appears beside the poem and can be redrawn.

## Emotions

Analysis now always scores these seven:

- Joy
- Sadness
- Anger
- Fear
- Surprise
- Disgust
- Neutral

Older names such as happiness, stress, calmness, and curiosity still map into this set in trends and history.

## Result layout

- The poem is the main result: a narrow slip on the left, sized for a later portable print.
- Emotion scores sit between the poem and the image.
- The generated image sits on the right.
- Cognitive reframing sits below that row.

## What do you need right now?

Each card now does what it says:

- **Release** opens the guided breathing pause.
- **Explore** takes a follow-up and reflects it as a new poem.
- **Reframe** moves to the reframing prompts under the poem.
- **Create** opens the drawing canvas.
- **Talk** opens the voice companion.

## Reflection styles

Settings → AI Reflection Style now includes:

- Warm
- More instructions
- Animals
- Fairy tales
- Lord of the Rings
- Matrix

The poem stays two stanzas of four lines. The style changes the voice. Film styles stay original and do not quote those movies.

## Appearance

Settings → Appearance is no longer locked to dark mode:

- **Dark** — the existing night theme
- **Light** — warm daylight
- **Romantic** — pink, with hearts

The choice is remembered.

## Other fixes

- Journal and Talk in the sidebar show their names instead of internal labels.
- Journal trends use the seven emotions above.
